Anyone with access to this browser profile can read it. Do not enable this on a shared or public machine, and prefer a key scoped with a spend limit.

About this tool

This is the automated extension of a manual architecture review board: instead of a human checking a proposed design against the roadmap and standards by hand, this reviewer reads a submitted architecture and flags violations (hard rule breaks), deviations needing an exception (soft/recommended patterns not followed, with no documented waiver), and Well-Architected gaps (required considerations not addressed at all) — citing the exact rule that fired. The rule pack in the Rule Library tab is meant to be replaced with your own enterprise patterns.

Runs entirely client-side · rule pack is user-editable · no data leaves your browser unless AI narrative is enabled
